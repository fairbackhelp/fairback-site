PIP appeal tribunal: what happens and how to prepare

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules change, so check your own letters and GOV.UK, including what you read here. Last checked: November 2026.

If your mandatory reconsideration doesn't change the decision, you can appeal to an independent tribunal. It is free and run by HM Courts & Tribunals Service, not DWP.

Deadline and form

Appeal within one month of the date on your mandatory reconsideration notice, online at GOV.UK or on form SSCS1.

The hearing

  • The panel is a judge, a doctor and a disability expert.
  • Choose a hearing (in person, phone or video) rather than a paper decision.
  • Describe a typical and a bad day, not your best day.
  • DWP sometimes offers a better award before the hearing. Get advice before you accept.
  • The tribunal looks at the whole award, so it can lower it as well as raise it. Get advice if you already have an award.

In October to December 2025, 64% of PIP appeals cleared at a hearing went the claimant's way (Ministry of Justice).
