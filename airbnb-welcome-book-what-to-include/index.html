A clear welcome book cuts guest messages and helps guests treat your home well. Keep it friendly and short at the front, with the detail behind.

What guests need first

  • A short welcome note.
  • Check-in time and how to get in; check-out time and what to do with keys.
  • Wi-fi name and password.
  • Your contact details and the best way to reach you.
  • Parking.

House rules

A few clear rules with the reason for each (quiet hours, smoking, pets, extra guests, bins). Guests follow rules they understand.

Using the home

  • Heating, hot water and the shower.
  • Oven, hob, washing machine, dishwasher and TV.
  • Where the first aid kit, torch and spare bedding are.
  • What to do in an emergency, and the local emergency number.

Local guide

Supermarket, late shop, pharmacy, urgent care, transport, places to eat and walks.

For you as the host

  • A before-arrival list: cleaning, linen, basics stocked, alarms tested, key code changed.
  • A check-out walk-round with an inventory and condition record, and dated photos of any damage.
  • Before you let your home, check your insurance, your mortgage lender or landlord, and your local council's short-let rules.

Leave private details (alarm codes, safe, bank details) out of the guest copy.
