Denial code 16 (CO-16): claim lacks information. How to get it paid

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules differ by plan and state, so check your own documents and official sources, including what you read here. Law last checked: 20 November 2026.

Short answer: code 16 means the claim was missing information or had a billing error. You often don't need to appeal. The provider corrects the claim and resubmits it.

What to do

  1. Ask the provider for an itemized bill with procedure (CPT) and diagnosis (ICD-10) codes.
  2. Compare it with your Explanation of Benefits (EOB). Look for wrong dates, duplicates, or a diagnosis that doesn't match the treatment.
  3. Ask the billing office to correct and resubmit, and to hold your account until the insurer reprocesses it.
  4. Check the new EOB in about 30 days. If the provider won't fix it, file an internal appeal with the insurer.
