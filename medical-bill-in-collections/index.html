Medical bill sent to collections? What to do first

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules differ by plan and state, so check your own documents and official sources, including what you read here. Law last checked: 20 November 2026.

Short answer: don't pay on the first call. You have more options than the collector will mention.

What to do

  1. Dispute in writing within 30 days of the collector's first notice and ask them to validate the debt. They must pause collection until they respond.
  2. Ask the hospital for an itemized bill and compare it with your EOB.
  3. Apply for financial assistance (charity care). Nonprofit hospitals must have a financial assistance policy and generally accept applications for at least 240 days after the first bill.
  4. If your insurance claim is still being appealed, tell the collector in writing.
  5. If you settle, get it in writing before paying, marked "paid in full".

The three big credit bureaus no longer report paid medical collections, or medical collections under $500.
