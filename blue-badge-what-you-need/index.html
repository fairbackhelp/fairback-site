Applying for a Blue Badge: what you need and how long it takes

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules change, so check your own letters and GOV.UK, including what you read here. Last checked: November 2026.

A Blue Badge helps disabled people park closer to where they need to go. You can apply for yourself, for someone else or for an organisation. Your local council decides.

What to have ready

  • A recent digital photo showing head and shoulders
  • A photo or scan of proof of identity (for example a birth certificate, passport or driving licence)
  • A photo or scan of proof of address (for example a Council Tax bill or a government letter)
  • A photo or scan of proof of benefits, if you get any
  • Your National Insurance number, if you have one
  • Details of your current Blue Badge, if you're reapplying

Cost

  • England: up to £10
  • Scotland: up to £20
  • Wales: free
  • Northern Ireland: apply through nidirect.gov.uk

Timing and renewal

GOV.UK says the council will usually decide within 12 weeks. A badge usually lasts up to 3 years, and you need to reapply before it expires. Put the expiry date somewhere you'll see it.

Keep copies

Keep copies of what you sent, the date you applied and any reference number, so you can follow up if you don't hear back.

Source: GOV.UK, "Apply for or renew a Blue Badge".
