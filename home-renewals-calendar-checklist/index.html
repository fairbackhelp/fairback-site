Many household costs renew once a year, and missing a date can mean an automatic price rise or a lapse in cover. A simple renewals calendar keeps every date in one place.

What to put on it

  • Insurance: home or renters, contents, car, pet, travel, life. Insurer, policy (last 4 digits) and renewal date
  • Contracts: energy, broadband, mobile phones, TV and streaming. End date and notice period
  • Subscriptions: software, memberships, deliveries. What it costs and when it renews
  • Servicing: boiler or furnace, chimney, car service and inspection
  • Licences and documents: passports, driving licence, vehicle registration, TV licence where it applies
  • Warranties: appliances and electronics, with the expiry date

How to use it

  • Note each date in the month it falls, plus a reminder a few weeks earlier
  • Before renewing, compare the new price with last year's
  • Keep it with your other home records, so someone else could find it in an emergency
