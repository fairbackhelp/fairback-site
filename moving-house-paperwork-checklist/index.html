Moving is easier when the paperwork is in one place. Use this as a starting list and add your own.

Before you move

  • Keep your contract or lease, inventory and deposit details together
  • Book removals and confirm dates in writing
  • Take meter photos on moving day (both homes)
  • Back up a list of every account that has your address

Who to tell

  • Banks, cards and pensions
  • Employer and tax office
  • Doctor, dentist and pharmacy
  • Insurance (home, car, health, pet)
  • Vehicle and driving licence records
  • Schools, subscriptions and deliveries
  • Mail forwarding

At the new home

  • Utilities and internet set up
  • Where the stopcock or water shut-off, fuse box and gas meter are
  • Appliance manuals and warranties
  • Contacts for landlord, agent or local repair people

Keep it for next time

A home information binder means the next person in your house, or the next move, starts with everything written down.
