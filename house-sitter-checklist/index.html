A good house-sitter can only be as good as the notes you leave. Most of the texts you get while away are questions a single page could have answered. Fill this in a week before you go, then walk the sitter round with it in hand.

The essentials

  • Address written as you would give it to a taxi or delivery driver, and how to find the door.
  • Keys: how many, which doors, and who holds a spare nearby.
  • Wi-fi name and password.
  • The local emergency number and a non-emergency line.
  • Your number, and a local person who can help if you can't be reached.

Where things are

  • Water stop tap, and how to turn it off.
  • Fuse box or circuit breakers.
  • Boiler or water heater, and the heating controls.
  • First aid kit, torch, spare bulbs, fire blanket or extinguisher.
  • Bins, recycling and the collection days.

If something goes wrong

Write the first step and who to call for: a leak, a power cut, a smell of gas, a fire alarm, being locked out, the heating stopping, and a break-in. The first step is what matters in the moment.

Before you leave

  • Check your home insurance covers someone staying, and how long the home can be left empty.
  • Agree in writing the dates, times, any payment, and how often you'll check in.
  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
  • Lock away private papers and valuables.
