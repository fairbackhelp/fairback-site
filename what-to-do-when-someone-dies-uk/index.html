What to do when someone dies in the UK: the first 30 days

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules change, so check your own letters and GOV.UK, including what you read here. Last checked: November 2026.

The first 48 hours

  • Expected death at home: call their GP or hospice (111 out of hours). Unexpected: call 999.
  • Contact a funeral director.
  • Register the death within 5 days in England, Wales and Northern Ireland (8 in Scotland).
  • At registration, ask for Tell Us Once and buy several certified copies of the death certificate.

Weeks one to four

  • Find the will and contact the executor.
  • Banks: the free Death Notification Service tells several at once.
  • Probate (England & Wales) or Confirmation (Scotland), applied for online.
  • Bereavement Support Payment: claim within 3 months for the full amount.
  • Find old accounts with My Lost Account and the Pension Tracing Service.
