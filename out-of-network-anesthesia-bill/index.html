Short answer: since 2022, the federal No Surprises Act protects you when you get care at an in-network hospital or surgery center and an out-of-network anesthesiologist, radiologist, pathologist, lab, assistant surgeon or ER doctor bills you. In those cases you generally owe only your in-network cost-sharing (copay, coinsurance or deductible).

What to do

  1. Check your EOB. It should show the claim processed at in-network cost-sharing. If not, appeal to your insurer.
  2. Write to the provider: cite the No Surprises Act, enclose the EOB, ask them to correct the bill and not send it to collections.
  3. Still billed? Contact the No Surprises Help Desk: 1-800-985-3059 and your state insurance department.

Note: these protections can't be waived for emergency care or for anesthesia, radiology, pathology, lab or assistant-surgeon services. Ground ambulance isn't covered by the federal law, though some states protect you.
