Home information binder: what to include (checklist)

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Laws and services differ by state and country, so check your own documents and official sources. Last checked: November 2026.

A home information binder (or household management binder) holds the facts about your home that nobody can find when they need them: where the water shut-off is, when the insurance renews, which model the dishwasher is.

The sections to include

  • Emergencies: water shut-off valve, gas shut-off, electrical panel, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, fire extinguisher, first aid kit, spare keys.
  • Household at a glance: address, who lives there, landlord or mortgage servicer, property tax account.
  • Utilities: electricity, gas, water, internet, phone. Provider, account (last 4) and when any deal ends.
  • Bills, autopay and subscriptions: amount, date and how each is paid, reviewed once a year.
  • Renewals calendar: insurance, vehicle registration, HVAC service and memberships, by month.
  • Appliances and warranties: brand, model and serial number, purchase date, warranty end. These help with repairs and recalls.
  • Maintenance schedule: test alarms monthly, service heating and cooling yearly, clean gutters and dryer vents.
  • Contractors and local services: plumber, electrician, doctor, vet, trash and recycling days.
  • Documents: where the deed or lease, insurance policies and home improvement records are kept.
  • Wi-Fi and smart home: router location and network name, and where the password is kept.

Keep it safe

Never write full passwords, PINs or account numbers in it. Note the last 4 digits and where the full details are kept.
