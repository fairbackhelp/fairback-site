Medication not covered or "not on formulary"? Ask for a formulary exception

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules differ by plan and state, so check your own documents and official sources, including what you read here. Law last checked: 20 November 2026.

Short answer: your doctor can ask the plan for a formulary exception (or a step-therapy exception), explaining why covered alternatives don't work or aren't safe for you. Most ACA plans must decide within 72 hours, or 24 hours if urgent.

What to do

  1. Ask the pharmacy for the exact reject message: "not on formulary", "prior auth required", "quantity limit" and "refill too soon" each have a different fix.
  2. Ask your doctor to file the exception with the reasons and your treatment history.
  3. Ask the drug maker about patient assistance or copay programs while you wait.
  4. If it's refused, file an internal appeal, then request external review.
