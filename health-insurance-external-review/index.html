Health insurance external review: how to get an independent decision

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules differ by plan and state, so check your own documents and official sources, including what you read here. Law last checked: 20 November 2026.

Short answer: if your insurer upholds a denial after your internal appeal, you can usually ask for an external review. An independent reviewer decides, and if they side with you, the plan must pay.

How it works

  • Request it within 4 months of the final internal denial for most private and employer plans.
  • Standard decisions take up to 45 days. Expedited reviews for urgent cases take about 72 hours.
  • Your final denial letter must tell you where to send the request: your insurer, your state insurance department, or the federal process.
  • Send the denial letters, your appeal, and supporting medical records.
