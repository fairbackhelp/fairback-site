Home maintenance checklist by season (printable)

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Laws and services differ by state and country, so check your own documents and official sources. Last checked: November 2026.

Small, regular jobs prevent most big repair bills. Adjust this list to your home and climate.

Every month

  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
  • Check or change heating and cooling filters (as your system's manual advises).
  • Run water in unused sinks and showers, and look under sinks for leaks.

Spring

  • Clean gutters and downspouts.
  • Service the air conditioning before hot weather.
  • Check the roof, windows and doors for winter damage.

Summer

  • Clean the dryer vent and duct.
  • Check outdoor paint, decks and fences.
  • Clean kitchen extractor and bathroom fans.

Fall / autumn

  • Service the heating system or boiler.
  • Clean gutters again after the leaves fall.
  • Drain and shut off outdoor taps where pipes can freeze.
  • Have a used chimney swept.

Winter

  • Know where the water shut-off valve is, in case a pipe bursts.
  • Keep a torch, spare batteries and a first aid kit where everyone can find them.
  • Check the fire extinguisher's gauge and expiry date.

Use qualified, licensed professionals for gas and electrical work.
