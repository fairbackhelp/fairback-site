Pets cope best when their routine stays the same. Write it down so your sitter doesn't have to guess, one page per pet, with a recent photo.

About each pet

  • Name, species, breed, age, colour and markings.
  • Microchip number and which database it is registered with. Check your contact details are up to date before you travel.
  • Health conditions, allergies, and things they must not eat.
  • Behaviour: fears, triggers, other animals, children, favourite hiding places.

The daily routine

  • Feeding times, food brand and exact amounts, and treats allowed.
  • Walks, play, litter, and bedtime.
  • Where food and supplies are kept, and where to buy more.

Medicines

Copy the dose and times from the label, show how to give it, and leave a simple log so nothing is missed or doubled. Follow your vet's instructions.

The vet

  • Your vet and the out-of-hours vet, with phone numbers and addresses.
  • Tell your vet who is caring for your pet, and agree how bills will be paid.
  • Pet insurance: insurer, policy (last 4 digits) and the claims number.

If your pet goes missing

Search the house and garden first, tell neighbours, then call the microchip database and local vets.
