PIP supporting evidence: what to include and how to keep track of it

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules change, so check your own letters and GOV.UK, including what you read here. Last checked: November 2026.

When you claim Personal Independence Payment (PIP), DWP sends a form called "How your disability affects you". GOV.UK says to return it, by post or online, within 1 month. If you need more time, contact the PIP enquiry line.

What GOV.UK says to include

"Include supporting documents if you have them", for example prescription lists, care plans, or information from your doctor or others involved in your care.

Keeping track of it

  • List every document you send: what it is, who it's from, the date, and the page that matters.
  • Keep copies of everything you send, and note the date you posted it or submitted it online.
  • Write down each call with DWP: the date, who you spoke to, and what was agreed.
  • Describe what happens on a typical day and a bad day, honestly and in your own words.

Free help: Citizens Advice and your council's welfare rights service. Source: GOV.UK, "PIP: how to claim".
