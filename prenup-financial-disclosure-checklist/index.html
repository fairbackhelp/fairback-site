If you're thinking about a prenuptial agreement, one thing comes up almost everywhere: each partner sharing an honest, full picture of their finances. Gathering it early makes the conversation and any lawyer's meetings easier. This page is a checklist, not legal advice.

What you own

  • Bank and savings accounts (institution, rough balance)
  • Pensions or retirement accounts
  • Property: homes, land, and what's still owed on them
  • Investments and shares
  • A business or a share in one
  • Vehicles and valuable items
  • Expected inheritances or trusts, if you know of them

What you owe

  • Mortgages and loans
  • Credit cards and overdrafts
  • Student loans
  • Car finance and any other debts

Income

  • Salary and other income, and recent tax returns or payslips

Good habits

  • Use recent statements, and note the date of each figure
  • Be open about debts as well as assets
  • If you decide on a formal agreement, each of you should speak to your own lawyer; the rules on disclosure and timing depend on where you live
