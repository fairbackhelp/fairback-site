Short answer: code 197 means the insurer had no prior authorization (precertification) on file for the service. It is usually a paperwork gap, not a judgement that you didn't need the care, and it is often fixed by the doctor's office.

What to do

  1. Don't pay the bill yet. Ask the provider's billing office to put your account on hold while it's sorted.
  2. Call the doctor's office first. Ask whether they submitted an authorization, and when.
  3. If they didn't, ask them to submit it now and request a retroactive authorization.
  4. If it's urgent, ask for an expedited review. Plans often decide urgent requests within 72 hours (some allow up to 4 business days, so check your notice).
  5. Once approved, ask the office to resubmit the claim. If the authorization is refused, you now have a medical necessity denial, which needs a different fix.

Deadline

For most private and employer plans you have 180 days from the denial notice to file an internal appeal. Medicare and Medicaid have different timelines, so follow the date on your notice.
