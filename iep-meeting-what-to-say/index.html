What to say at an IEP meeting: 12 phrases that get you heard

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules differ by plan and state, so check your own documents and official sources, including what you read here. Law last checked: 20 November 2026.

Short answer: you are an equal member of your child's IEP team. Polite, specific phrases help you get your concerns on the record.

Phrases you can use

  • "I would like my concerns written into the IEP, please."
  • "Can you show me the data behind that?"
  • "What is the baseline for this goal, and how was it chosen?"
  • "Who will provide this service, and how many minutes a week?"
  • "Can you explain that without the technical terms?"
  • "I need time to review the draft before I agree."
  • "What does my signature mean on this page?"
  • "Please give me Prior Written Notice explaining that decision."
  • "I disagree. Please note my disagreement in the meeting notes."
  • "What other options did the team consider, and why were they rejected?"
  • "Can we put the next steps and dates in writing before we finish?"
  • "When will I receive a copy of the final IEP?"

Rules and timelines vary by state. Every state has a free Parent Training and Information Center: find yours at parentcenterhub.org.
