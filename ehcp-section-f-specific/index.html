Is section F of the EHCP specific enough? Vague vs specific examples

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules change, so check your own letters and GOV.UK, including what you read here. Last checked: November 2026.

Section F lists the special educational provision your child must get. It is the legally enforceable part of the plan, so wording matters. The SEND Code of Practice (para 9.69) says provision should be detailed, specific and normally quantified.

Vague vs specific

  • Vague: "Access to speech and language therapy." Specific: "1 hour of direct speech and language therapy each week, delivered by a qualified SALT, plus a termly review."
  • Vague: "Adult support as needed." Specific: "15 hours a week of 1:1 support from a TA trained in autism."
  • Watch for "may", "should", "access to" and "opportunities for".

Respond to the draft

You have 15 days from receiving the draft plan to comment and name a school. Ask for every vague item to say what, how much, how often and who.
