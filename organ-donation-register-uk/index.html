Organ donation in the UK: the opt-out system and how to record your decision

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules change, so check your own letters and GOV.UK, including what you read here. Last checked: November 2026.

England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland all now have an opt-out system for organ donation. In general, adults are considered to have agreed to donate unless they have recorded a decision not to, or are in an excluded group. The details differ slightly in each nation, so check the NHS Organ Donation website for yours.

Your choices

  • Record a decision to donate (all organs, or only some).
  • Record a decision not to donate (opt out).

You can record or change your decision at any time on the NHS Organ Donor Register (organdonation.nhs.uk) or by calling 0300 123 23 23.

Tell your family

Families are still involved, so it helps to tell the people closest to you what you have decided.

Source: NHS Blood and Transplant, organdonation.nhs.uk. This page is information only. Whatever you decide is your choice.
