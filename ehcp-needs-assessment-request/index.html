How to request an EHC needs assessment (EHCP) yourself

Self-help information, for educational purposes only. Not legal, medical or financial advice. Rules change, so check your own letters and GOV.UK, including what you read here. Last checked: November 2026.

In England, you don't need the school's agreement to ask for an Education, Health and Care (EHC) needs assessment. A parent can write to the council directly (Children and Families Act 2014, section 36).

The deadlines

  • The council must decide whether to assess within 6 weeks.
  • It must decide whether to issue a plan within 16 weeks.
  • You get 15 days to comment on a draft plan.
  • The final plan is due within 20 weeks of your request.

What to write

Say your child has or may have special educational needs, and that they may need provision through an EHC plan. Briefly describe the needs, what support has been tried, and attach any reports. Keep the date you sent it: the 6-week clock starts then.

If the council refuses, you can appeal to the SEND Tribunal. In 2023–24, 95% of appeals that reached a decision went in favour of families (Ministry of Justice).
